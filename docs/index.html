What it does

The panels show what the cockpit shows

DCS Signal Converter reads the cockpit through DCS-BIOS and drives the lamps, backlights and screens on your WinWing panels to match. It starts with DCS on its own, so there is nothing to remember before a flight and no alt-tabbing when you change aircraft.

Lamps

Gear, Master Caution, APU fire, jettison and the rest light when the cockpit's do, from either seat in a two-seater.

Backlights

Every panel backlight shares one setting, held steady so the pit stays readable. Point it at a cockpit knob and they all dim together.

Screens

The Hornet UFC on the UFC, the Viper's DED on the ICP, and on your MCDU or PFP the A-10C, CH-47F and F-14B (Upgrade) CDU, the AH-64D KU, the Hornet's IFEI or a Viper flight page. Every screen takes pages you build yourself: write a label, put a reading beside it, and pick the colour and size of each.

Setup

Five steps, once

01Install DCS-BIOS into "Saved Games\DCS\Scripts"
02Copy the line that ships with DCS-BIOS into "Scripts\Export.lua"
03Close DCS
04Run the installer from the latest release
05Start DCS and fly
Then it just runs

A small hook starts the converter with each mission. Within a second or two of the cockpit loading it picks your aircraft's profile and sets every lamp to match. Leave the mission and the panels go dark; quit DCS and it quits too.

Running SimAppPro alongside

The converter can run with SimAppPro open, but SimAppPro must stop driving the same panels. Set the lights on each device in SimAppPro not to sync with DCS. For the F-16, set every ICP page selection to disabled; for the F/A-18, set every UFC page selection to disabled. Leaving them on will not harm your devices, but the two apps will fight over the panels, causing extra writes and potentially strange visuals.

The editor

Every lamp is yours to reassign, per aircraft

Every test, reading and page the editor offers is explained, with examples to try, in the profile language.

Proven on the panel

Any screen, filled with whatever you choose

An aircraft with no CDU of its own leaves the MCDU free. Pick the readings, type the labels, set each one's colour, size and width, and it draws live from DCS. No code, no files to edit by hand.

Starts with DCS

A hook launches it with each mission. Nothing to start by hand, and SimAppPro can stay closed.

Follows you mid-session

Join mid-mission or switch slots and the panels catch up within a couple of seconds. Nothing to restart.

Swap pages

Give the MCDU, the UFC and the ICP's DED up to six pages each. Hold Ctrl and press one of the panel's page keys to put that page on the screen, mid-flight.

Edit in flight

Save in the editor and the running converter picks it up within about a second. Watch the lamp change without leaving the cockpit.

Share it with a friend

Export a profile to a file, send it over, and they import it, its pages with it. It is checked before it lands, and refused if it would not load on their machine. Or merge in only the lamps, page slots and pages you want.

Clean exit

Quit DCS and the panels go dark. If DCS crashes, the converter notices the silence, clears the panels and exits on its own.

Touches nothing else

Runs unelevated. No DCS, DCS-BIOS, SimAppPro or WinWing file is modified.

Your changes are kept

Updates add new profiles, panels and pages but never rewrite a lamp you have changed. A page or slot still exactly as it shipped is corrected for you; one you have touched is yours and is left alone. Release notes say which shipped rows moved.

Before you start

What you need

DCS-BIOS
Required. It is where every signal comes from. Stable or nightly both work; the few lamps and fields that need a newer DCS-BIOS are named in the editor, and come back on their own when you update it.
WinWing panels
Any of the panels above. The same hardware sold as WinCtrl is the same device and works the same.
Not SimAppPro
It does not need to be running, and can stay closed. If you do run it, turn off Sync with DCS for each panel so the two do not fight over the backlights. In the Hornet and the Viper it also draws the UFC and the DED itself; to leave those to SimAppPro, untick drive this panel for that panel in the profile, or set its page slots to Disabled.
Read this bit: it is an alpha

It is flown daily on the author's panels, but WinWing do not publish how their panels talk to the PC, so the USB protocol was reverse-engineered, and the installer is new. Treat it as community software rather than a vendor feature.

An independent project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by WinCtrl or WinWing; their names appear here only to say which hardware it drives. Bug reports very welcome.
